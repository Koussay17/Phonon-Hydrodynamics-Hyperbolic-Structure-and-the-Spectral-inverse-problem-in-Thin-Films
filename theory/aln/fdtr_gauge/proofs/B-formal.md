```lean
theorem step5 {a b c d : ℝ}
    (h : ∀ p : ℝ, (1 + a * p) * (1 + d * p) = (1 + c * p) * (1 + b * p)) :
    (a ≠ b → c = a ∧ d = b) ∧ (a = b → c = d)
```

`Fib bs θ` encodes $\mathfrak F(\theta)$ exactly as in Theorem 1 (with $\Theta$, $\Delta$, $D_2$ of Definition 1),
and `mem_fibre_iff_of_not_D2` states: for $\theta\in\Theta\setminus D_2$ and $\theta'\in\Theta$,

Differences from the informal statement:
1. The informal hypothesis is "$g=g'$ as rational (meromorphic) functions". It appears in three
   forms: as a polynomial identity for all real $p$ (`step5`, `step5_iff`); as equality on

   is stronger than needed.
2. `step5` assumes no sign condition (the conclusion holds for all real times). `step5_g` uses only

   `step4` instead reads off the constant term of a quadratic identity in $p$ from $p=1,2,3$. Its

### 3.5 Provenance of the coefficients (independent route, not in `B-analytic.md`)

From Lemma 1, $dZ/dA=(1-B^2Z^2)/B$. With Lemma 0 ($AB=\xi_1bp$, $A/B=(\xi_1/b)g$) this gives
$\partial_{\xi_1}Z=g/b-bpZ^2$ with $Z|_{\xi_1=0}=L$ (`hasDerivAt_Zfilm`, `hasDerivAt_Zfilm_model`, over $\mathbb C$).

$$\partial_{\xi_1}q_{n+1}=[n\text{ even}]\,\gamma_{n/2}/b-b\textstyle\sum_{j=0}^nq_jq_{n-j},\qquad q_{n+1}(0)=0\ (R=0).$$
* `hierarchy` and `hierarchy_init`: $c_0,\dots,c_6$ satisfy it.
* `hierarchy_unique`: any family of functions of $\xi_1$ satisfying it on $\mathbb R$ with these initial data
  equals $(q_k)_{k\le7}$.
* `gam_series`: the $\gamma_m$ are the Taylor coefficients of $g$.

SymPy confirms the same route independently of `laurent.py`, up to $c_7$ and with a contact resistance

Granted that standard step, the coefficients used in §3.3 are the Laurent coefficients of the model.
