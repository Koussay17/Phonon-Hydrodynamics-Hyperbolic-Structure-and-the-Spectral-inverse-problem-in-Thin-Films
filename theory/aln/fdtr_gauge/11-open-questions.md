## In-plane film quantities: mesh artefact of the |v_z|/L regulator (found by branch A-C, 11 November)
On a uniform mesh the plane q_z = 0 has v_z = 0 exactly and is never suppressed by the regulator; it carries 944/N_z W/mK of
κ_x(L) at every L (24 % of κ_x(30 nm) on 64×64×35, about 118 W/mK on 12×12×8). In-plane film values from the event operator are
biased; the cross-plane quantities used in E33/E37 (τ_z, κ_z) are not affected. To check: any in-plane film statement of
fc3-memory E30 (5×5×3 has N_z = 3, a third of the points on that plane).
