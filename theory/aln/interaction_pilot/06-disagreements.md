The apparent contradiction between accurate diagonalization and unequal periodic frequencies was resolved at matrix level: the assembled NAC matrices differ. A fixed-Lambda reciprocal-cutoff sweep removes the tested discrepancy to roundoff, supporting truncation at this pair.
A conditional physical canonical coefficient and C's refusal to certify canonical conversion concern different assumptions: A requires correctly unfolded and counted events, which this export has not supplied. Keep canonical counting open.
Scalar reciprocity and accumulation agreement coexist with strong width dependence. These test different propositions; neither establishes continuum convergence.
The original periodicity failure remains factual. A corrected complete interaction export and its effect on pp/gamma remain untested.
