# Pilot assumptions and scope
Reuse one entire pinned force-constant family with its own structure/BORN.
Cubic supercell 3x3x2, harmonic supercell 5x5x3 as author README.
Tiny mesh 3x3x3, selected grid point 1, 300 K, two worker threads.
Gaussian width 0.1 THz is an explicit pilot integration setting, not a
converged physical linewidth or proof of exact discrete energy conservation.
No conductivity conclusion is drawn from one grid point.
Do not modify force constants to make unexpected results look physical.
