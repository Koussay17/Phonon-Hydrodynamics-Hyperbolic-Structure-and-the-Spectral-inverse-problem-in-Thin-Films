# Independent branches and second generation
First passes were isolated: A physical channel/Bose counting; B finite-group orbit enumeration; C dimensional/source normalization; D numerical integration and conditioning. Their original reports are in branches/.
Second generation used the distinctions between scalar accounting and canonical event counting to test direct pp-to-gamma accumulation, integer orbit partitions, reciprocal points, Gaussian widths, and harmonic matrices.
Four independent attacks followed: counterexamples, proof/units, numerical reliability, peer review. Their reports are preserved without retroactive editing. The later cutoff control responds to the unresolved numerical defect; it does not supersede original measurements.
A's canonical coefficient is conditional and is not accepted as a validated importer. Agreement of agents is not evidence beyond their derivations, sources and tests.
