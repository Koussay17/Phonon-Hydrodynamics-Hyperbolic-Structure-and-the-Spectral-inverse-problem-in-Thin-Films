# Hypotheses after attack

H1 is accepted only in the stated broad PSD class: fixed diagonal data do not in general identify response or memory. Actual microscopic AlN nonidentifiability has not been proved.

H2 is derived under explicit fast-coercivity and coordinate assumptions; a global gap need not exist in the continuum. Observable-specific alternatives remain conditional.

H3 is supported by exact countermodels and observed concentration of finite-grid inverse moments. Actual AlN continuum memory divergence is UNKNOWN, not inferred.

The stronger conjecture of unbounded memory at bounded DC in one fixed finite nonnegative event cone is DISPROVED by the audited event-cone bound.

Four discovery proposals are retained only as conditional research directions; each has explicit rejection gates in discovery-attacks.md. No major implementation followed them.
