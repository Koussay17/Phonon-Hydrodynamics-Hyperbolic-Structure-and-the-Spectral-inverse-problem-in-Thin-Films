# Assumptions governing accepted claims

- Linear response at fixed positive temperature; entropy-normalized coordinates.
- Finite-dimensional real symmetric PSD collision operators; exact invariants and nonzero currents explicitly specified.
- The broad PSD class is NOT the microscopic AlN event cone.
- Event-cone bound: finite fixed dictionary, fixed inner product, fixed dissipative space and current, nonnegative weights, positive-definite restriction. No uniformity under mesh or temperature changes.
- Schur estimate: orthogonal split, Hermitian streaming, fast block D >= gamma I, Re(z) >= 0; inverse bound needs Re(z) > 0. Initial/source terms retained.
- Infrared thresholds: fixed positive T, uniform power-law value asymptotics, nondegenerate current support and angular rate coefficient bounded away from zero. Pointwise density additionally requires derivative control or a separate measure-theoretic proof.
- Film diagnostics: stationary in-plane RTA, reflecting surfaces; not cross-plane FDTR.
- One physical q mesh: no continuum convergence or uncertainty bound follows from independent summation.
