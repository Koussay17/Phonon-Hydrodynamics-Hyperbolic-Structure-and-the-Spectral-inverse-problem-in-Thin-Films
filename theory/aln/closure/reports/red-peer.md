## Verdict

**The expressly limited eight-dimensional PSD-matrix claim was not falsified. Any promotion of that claim to a fixed microscopic AlN event network must be rejected.** There is a concrete structural obstruction beyond the report's statement that physical realization has not been constructed: the entire D family approaching t=0 cannot belong to any single cone generated by finitely many fixed positive-semidefinite scattering-event matrices. Its required coupling scales as t while the corresponding dissipation scales as t². A fixed nonnegative event library bounds the ratio of these quantities.

This obstruction does not refute the frozen broad-class theorem. It does refute one natural proposed physical realization of its mechanism and prevents using D alone to establish unbounded memory for an actual fixed phonon dataset. The finite-class counterexample is useful as a warning about insufficient assumptions. Its significance and novelty as a top-journal result remain unestablished.

## 1. Major issue: the physically admissible collision cone is smaller

### Exact proposition under attack

The possible inference is: frequencies, velocities, collision diagonals, and the DC tensor fixed for a physical crystal still permit arbitrarily large memory, as witnessed by D. D explicitly disclaims this inference; the campaign must preserve that restriction in every conclusion.

### Primary-source structure

Fugallo et al., *Ab initio variational approach for evaluating lattice thermal conductivity*, PRB 88, 045430 (2013), Appendix A, Eqs. (31)–(34), write the collision matrix as a sum of nonnegative event probabilities multiplying fixed rank-one PSD matrices. Three-phonon events have the signed triplet vector (1,1,-1); isotope events have the pair vector (1,-1). A fixed entropy-coordinate congruence preserves this structure. This is stronger than merely requiring the total matrix to be PSD. [Actual author manuscript inspected](https://arxiv.org/html/1212.0470v2#A1).

Spohn's independent collision quadratic form contains a nonnegative event weight multiplying the square of the three-phonon imbalance, with explicit energy/momentum constraints; see Eq. (4.1). [Actual primary manuscript inspected](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0706.0815).

### Reviewer-derived finite-library obstruction

**Assumptions:** a finite-dimensional real mode space; a finite fixed list of event vectors v_a; nonnegative weights rho_a(t); fixed temperature, harmonic data, entropy coordinates, and quadrature. Write

    C(t) = sum_a rho_a(t) v_a v_a^T,   rho_a(t) >= 0.

For any two fixed vectors z,b, set

    R(z,b) = max_{a: z^T v_a != 0} |(b^T v_a)/(z^T v_a)|.

The maximum is finite because the library is finite. If the index set is empty, both z^T C z and z^T C b vanish. Otherwise,

    |z^T C(t) b|
      <= sum_a rho_a(t) |z^T v_a| |b^T v_a|
      <= R(z,b) sum_a rho_a(t) (z^T v_a)^2
       = R(z,b) z^T C(t) z.                         (R1)

For the frozen D family, take z=h1 and b=h2. The displayed B block gives

    z^T C(t) z = t²,       z^T C(t) b = t.

Consequently any representation by that one fixed finite event library would require

    1/t <= R(z,b).

This fails along every sequence t -> 0. Equivalently, the tangent direction of D at its rank-changing endpoint violates the finite-library inequality. This argument does not use fixed diagonals, so that extra constraint cannot remove the obstruction.

**Evidence status:** complete elementary conditional derivation by this reviewer; not formally verified and not independently proof-audited. The PI should obtain proof audit before calling it PROVED. The source supplies the event decomposition; inequality (R1) and its application are this reviewer's inference, not a theorem attributed to the cited papers.

**Exact scope:** this excludes the entire D path near zero for any one finite fixed event library, including a fixed finite phonon mesh with standard nonnegative event rates and fixed entropy weights. It does not exclude one isolated member of D; another physical family with unbounded memory; an infinite event library; a continuum/mesh limit with unbounded R; or a family that changes the harmonic data, coordinate transformation, or event vectors. It does not establish a universal upper bound for all physically admissible operators.

**Severity:** fatal to interpreting D itself as a realizable fixed-input microscopic unbounded-memory family. Not fatal to its expressly broad PSD theorem. The microscopic issue is therefore stronger than an absent worked example.

## 2. Additional direct obstruction for the natural equal-weight Bose interpretation

Suppose the eight coordinates are actual positive-frequency phonon modes on an equal-weight full-zone grid, at one finite positive temperature, with exact Bose statistics. Then the normalized energy-vector entries satisfy

    e_i proportional to epsilon_i sqrt(n_i(1+n_i))
        = k_B T x_i / [2 sinh(x_i/2)],
    x_i = epsilon_i/(k_B T) > 0.

This energy vector is given explicitly by Simoncelli et al., Appendix A.1, Eq. (30). [Actual author manuscript inspected](https://arxiv.org/html/1906.09743v2#A1.SS1).

The function x/[2 sinh(x/2)] is strictly decreasing for x>0: with u=x/2, its derivative has the sign of sinh(u)-u cosh(u)<0, since tanh(u)<u. Equal e_i therefore forces equal positive frequencies. No exact three-phonon resonance epsilon_i+epsilon_j=epsilon_k is then possible within this isolated eight-mode set. Remaining isotope events have nonpositive off-diagonal entries after a positive diagonal entropy congruence. Direct symbolic reconstruction of D gives

    C_04(t) = t/2 > 0.

Thus that particular direct eight-mode three-phonon-plus-isotope realization is impossible for every t>0.

**Scope limits:** unequal quadrature weights, a classical equipartition approximation, extra modes, four-phonon events, or a coarse-grained nonpopulation basis change this argument. They must be stated and checked; none is supplied by D. Positive off-diagonals alone are not unphysical for genuine three-phonon collision matrices. The obstruction uses the additional equal-energy deduction, not a false general graph-sign condition.

## 3. Major issue: divergent DC slope does not establish a large measurable signal

D defines

    tau_mem = -K_t'(0)/K_t(0),
    K_t(p) = (p+t²)/[p²+(2+t²)p+t²].

This is a normalized first temporal moment of the current-response kernel. It is not automatically a momentum relaxation time, viscosity, second-sound decay time, Mori friction-memory time, or FDTR fitting parameter.

An exact subtraction yields

    K_t(p) - 1/(p+2)
      = t² / [(p+2)(p²+(2+t²)p+t²)].               (R2)

For real p >= p_min > 0,

    0 <= K_t(p) - 1/(p+2)
       <= t²/[p_min(p_min+2)²].

Hence the response converges uniformly on every such band while tau_mem diverges. On a harmonic band |omega| >= omega_min > 0, factoring the denominator into the two positive B eigenvalues gives

    |K_t(i omega)-1/(i omega+2)|
       <= t²/[omega_min(omega_min²+4)],

because lambda_+ >= 2 and |i omega+lambda_-| >= |omega|. The result also holds for the conjugate convention.

**What this rejects:** an inference that divergent tau_mem must produce an arbitrarily large fixed-bandwidth response difference. The finite-frequency response can approach a regular limit. The singular window is p=O(t²); neither an accurate response at p=0.3 nor a fixed minimum experimental frequency resolves its slope. D acknowledges the noncommuting limits, but that caveat must survive summaries.

There is no evidence here of a propagating hydrodynamic mode: the uniform collision response consists only of decaying real modes. A large time moment does not identify their momentum content or finite-wavevector propagation.

## 4. Prior art and significance

The scientifically meaningful question is whether realistic data constrain a controlled anisotropic closure. The broad observations that full collision eigenvectors matter and that kinetic response is governed by an inverse operator are established. Spohn derives current correlations and inverse-operator conductivity in Eqs. (3.23)–(3.24), discusses event constraints, zero modes, and spectral gaps, and explicitly distinguishes RTA. This is particularly relevant primary literature absent from the frozen L report. [Spohn manuscript](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0706.0815).

Fugallo et al. supply both the variational solution and the event-level PSD decomposition. Simoncelli et al. derive tensor viscosity and temperature/drift equations with odd/even sector distinctions. These sources make it inappropriate to present collective relaxation, off-diagonal coupling, or spectral closure as new.

No exact earlier eight-mode fixed-diagonal/fixed-DC construction was established in this bounded search. That is not novelty evidence. The general inverse-moment separation follows immediately from a small current weight approaching zero rate; the potentially distinctive element is satisfying the additional finite matrix constraints. Its publication significance depends on an unresolved physical or inverse-problem consequence beyond the example itself.

The search was deliberately bounded to the verdict. Hua–Lindsay arXiv HTML and a Nature primary page failed to open in this pass; no full-text conclusion is attributed to those failed fetches. No exhaustive moment-problem, electrical-network, inverse-system, or matrix-cone prior-art search was completed. No novelty conclusion is defensible.
