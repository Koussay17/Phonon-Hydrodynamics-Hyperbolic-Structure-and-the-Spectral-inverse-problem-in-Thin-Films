# Question
How far can a quantitative, conserving, anisotropic hydrodynamic closure for real AlN be derived and identified from the currently available spectral data? What additional collision information and convergence tests are indispensable?

## Mathematical formulation
Linearized entropy-coordinate kinetic equation: dy/dt + V dot grad(y) = -C y + source. Distinguish the exact collision operator from any projected diagonal surrogate. Seek controlled elimination of fast modes, effective conductivity/memory/viscosity and a validity test. Determine identifiability from frequencies, velocities and total diagonal rates alone.

## Domain and physical interpretation
Bulk periodic crystal for operator derivations; planar film only with explicitly specified reflecting or transmitting boundary conditions. Small deviations about uniform temperature. Initial perturbations finite energy; harmonic forcing uses a causal Laplace resolvent. No assertion that an in-plane slab represents cross-plane FDTR.

## Parameters and dimensionless groups
Temperature, thickness, rates, wave vector and frequency, collision spectral gaps; omega*tau, |k|v/gap, projected mean free path/thickness. First data set is 300 K only.

## Success and falsification
A justified closure with stated error/regime, or a constructive non-identifiability result and reproducible next-data specification. Falsification: operators matching supplied observables but giving incompatible hydrodynamic dynamics; absent gap; divergent moments; boundary mismatch.
