# Conserving resonance measure: precise question

Can a finite representation of the three-phonon collision integral approximate the continuum energy-resonance measure while preserving nonnegative entropy production, Bose detailed balance and energy conservation, without pretending that broadened off-shell mesh tuples are exactly resonant?

## Formulation
Branches s have positive energy epsilon_s(q) on a periodic Brillouin zone; address acoustic zeros separately. Momentum conservation eliminates one daughter momentum modulo a reciprocal vector. For decay p->a+b define Delta=epsilon_p-epsilon_a-epsilon_b. Continuum integrals involve a nonnegative interaction weight and delta(Delta). The linearized weak form contains an integral of w*(psi_p-psi_a-psi_b)^2 over resonant triples.
At finite resolution choose explicitly the representation of energy, occupations, entropy variable, interpolation and quadrature. Test positivity, exact/controlled invariant defects, detailed balance, normalization, and convergence. An exactly positive matrix is not automatically a convergent physical operator.

## Domain and physical regime
Periodic bulk reciprocal space; positive finite temperature, weak anharmonic coupling and a declared kinetic approximation. No film boundaries or experimental geometry in this stage. Start with regular smooth resonant surfaces with nonzero gradient and separately investigate critical/empty/degenerate resonance sets. Neither finite isolated Hamiltonian irreversibility nor arbitrary material scalar closure is assumed proved.

## Parameters and diagnostics
Mesh scale h, energy width sigma, temperature, minimum group-gradient magnitude, coupling/observation-time scales; compare sigma to local energy variation h*|grad Delta|. State order of limits and units. No universal coupled scaling assumed.
