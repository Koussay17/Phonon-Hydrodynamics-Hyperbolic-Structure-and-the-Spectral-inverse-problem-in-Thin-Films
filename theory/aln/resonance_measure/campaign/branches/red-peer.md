**Verdict:** I find no fatal counterexample to the stated local finite identities. I would reject their promotion to a convergent AlN collision method or an original material-physics result. Correct finite thermodynamic structure can coexist with an arbitrarily wrong collision measure and relaxation spectrum. The frozen candidate acknowledges this limitation; it must remain central to any subsequent interpretation.

## Strongest parts and evidence classification

- **PROVED under the stated finite assumptions:** positive capacity, local well-posedness, quadrature energy conservation and entropy production, Bose stationarity, and the restricted positive-weight obstruction. The decisive identity is e^T K e = sum omega Lambda (b e)^2. Smooth positive Bose factors and the logarithmic mean provide the local ODE regularity. These statements establish neither a continuum limit nor a kinetic derivation.
- **NUMERICALLY DEMONSTRATED in the supplied record, not replayed here:** regular analytic quadratures, failed Gaussian sampling limits, the occupation-interpolation counterexample and C2 derivative/arithmetic checks.
- **UNSUPPORTED by this campaign:** global realizability, trajectory/spectral convergence, a converged AlN measure, or a validated AlN scalar closure. These stronger statements are explicitly withheld. Physical validity of the proposed alternative models remains **SPECULATIVE**. Nothing is formally verified or experimentally supported here.

## Fatal issues, if any

None found for the restricted theorem. The absent physical measure, approximation theorem and material regime are fatal to treating it as an accepted material collision operator. No failure of the local theorem should be inferred merely because these broader questions remain open.

## Major issues

1. **Conservation does not constrain the correct rates.** Multiply all reaction weights by any positive constant: energy conservation, entropy sign, Bose stationarity and the energy null vector persist while every collision time changes. Set the reaction list empty: the same structural checks pass. C/D give less trivial failures with wrong Jacobians or missed roots. The decisive missing result is consistency of the collision action on a sufficiently rich class of non-invariant functions, followed by stability under refinement. Fixed-smooth-integrand quadrature convergence does not supply convergence for an evolving, basis-dependent Bose reconstruction or its inverse collision operator.

2. **The physical regime excludes precisely the difficult material sectors.** The construction assumes a scalar, factorized, Markov kinetic model with positive smooth energies and regular resonances. There is no AlN comparison of coherence/dephasing scales, linewidths, branch splittings, or weighted infrared/critical contributions. Excluding acoustic zeros, crossings and critical sets is a model restriction until their omitted contribution is bounded. A regular joint surface may remove a singular fixed-parent chart while leaving an unbounded pointwise rate; it does not establish the spectrum relevant to transport. Resonance criticality and its dimension-dependent consequences are established prior concerns, not resolved by this finite algebra ([Shi and Eyink, sections 1 and 3](https://arxiv.org/html/1507.08320v1)).

3. **The representation changes what is evolved.** Fractional event rows redistribute retained moments, not labelled integer phonon populations. Full volume rank guarantees a local coordinate map only. Neither off-grid positivity nor a uniform capacity bound follows from the energy and entropy identities. The candidate explicitly leaves global domain preservation unproved. Thus the finite theorem cannot yet justify running a dynamical material model for a prescribed physical time, even if every reaction point is exactly resonant. This is an unverified extension, not a demonstrated finite-time failure.

4. **The obstruction must not become a criticism of all broadening physics.** Its sign proof assumes unchanged bare energies, conventional incidence and one common forward/reverse rate. It rejects positive reweighting inside that class. A spectral or finite-time theory may account for interaction/correlation energy or change the retained variables; its legitimate conservation target need not be the bare harmonic energy used here. Conversely, projecting a mobility or imposing an entropy-constrained step can enforce these finite identities while inventing different kinetics. None of those alternatives is physically selected by the present tests. The candidate's restrictions are essential.

5. **Continuum surface nodes are not automatically finite oscillator events.** A2 correctly identifies the remaining discontinuity in the import logic: identical-daughter labels can form a zero-measure subset of a transverse regular continuum surface, while a quadrature node on that subset represents a finite surrounding region. Attaching the finite repeated-oscillator factor solely from that node's coincident coordinates can change the continuum quadrature. The previous channel-counting identities and this measure construction therefore cannot be joined by a label-based counting shortcut. No globally enumerated AlN implementation tests this distinction.

6. **Verification covers identities, not the advertised application difficulty.** The regular examples have analytic roots or simple geometry. No certified multidimensional component coverage, material branch sewing, periodic orbit enumeration, singular weighted integral, or independent refinement of basis/volume/surface rules was demonstrated. C2 integrates no trajectory. Its initial failed assertion was replaced by a justified allowance involving the measured difference between b(beta e) and beta(b e); source inspection confirms the correction is an arithmetic consistency diagnosis. Because the allowance uses the same computed affinities and mobilities, passing it is not an independent bound on root error or physical heating accuracy. No formal rounding guarantee was established.

## Minor issues

- In note 23, “integrable f” is insufficient if it means only ambient-volume L1 integrability: evaluating a specified zero-level surface requires a defined integrable trace. The branch derivations use stronger regularity, so this is a formulation issue rather than a counterexample to their smooth benchmarks.
- Entropy-variable Galerkin, invariant test spaces, implicit-surface quadrature and resonance singularities have substantial prior art documented by L. No general novelty is claimed, and this bounded review does not establish priority for their Bose combination.
- Primary access limitation: I inspected Shi–Eyink directly; the Abdelmalik–van Brummelen DOI returned an access error in this review. Its detailed attribution is supported here by L's recorded full-paper inspection, not a fresh independent reading. No exhaustive historical search or clean-environment replay was undertaken.

## Decisive tests

Broader acceptance requires separately falsifiable evidence for: complete, correctly normalized resonance coverage; approximation of non-invariant collision actions and spectra under independent refinements; global realizability or a specified finite-time validity bound; and material estimates for the excluded acoustic/coherent/critical sectors. A weak-form example with exact invariants alone cannot decide any of these. These are missing acceptance tests, not calculations performed in this review.
