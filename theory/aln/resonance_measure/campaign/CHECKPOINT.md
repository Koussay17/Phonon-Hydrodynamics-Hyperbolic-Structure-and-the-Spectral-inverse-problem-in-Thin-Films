# Checkpoint before hostile-review synthesis

1. Learned: resonance measure, finite energy representation and statistical reconstruction are separate requirements. The first two rounds provide explicit discriminating tests.
2. Strongest candidates: positive off-shell thermal-drift obstruction and finite entropy-variable identities under stated domains; acceptance awaits proof review.
3. Weakened claims: invariants alone certify quadrature; small energy drift certifies convergence; source integration weights are unique resonant events.
4. Dominant assumptions: exact/represented energy compatibility, positive quadratures, full rank and positive reconstructed entropy variable, regular surface coverage, declared scalar kinetic model.
5. Unresolved distinctions: continuum versus quadrature energy; physical versus surrogate dispersion; local finite structure versus global dynamics; parent-slice versus joint criticality.
6. Most informative next tests: independent root-chart coverage and shifted meshes; separate basis/volume/surface refinement; actual dispersion patch with controlled excluded remainder. Review may change priorities.
7. Repeated failed approaches to avoid: broadening as roundoff, positive weight fitting off shell, occupation interpolation, sign-change scans as empty-set certificates, wrong Jacobian.
8. Literature impact: established entropic Galerkin and implicit-surface methods constrain originality claims. Classical results do not automatically validate Bose material kinetics.
9. Leading hypothesis: a compatible finite entropy moment scheme is worth bounded audit, but not yet a basis for a full material implementation.
10. Resources: pause expansion for four independent attacks. Do not invest in projected/spectral alternatives before their counterexample review.

Strongest remaining reason the candidate could be wrong: a hidden mismatch between the discrete energy/entropy representation and the claimed invariant, or a numerical quadrature with incomplete geometry, may evade algebraic checks. Even a correct finite construction may approximate the wrong physical model.
