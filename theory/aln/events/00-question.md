# Physical collision-event validation
Question: Can an explicitly counted reciprocal pair of exactly resonant three-phonon events produce a validated full-population entropy-coordinate collision action, and what remains necessary to import real AlN events?
Scope: finite positive-frequency modes on an equal-weight full grid; finite positive temperature; homogeneous linearization about Bose equilibrium; no spatial boundaries or forcing in the first validation.
Unknowns: event counting, population-to-entropy normalization, repeated-index factors, parity, broadened nonresonant events and material export.
Acceptance: independently derive linearization, compare with nonlinear Bose collision finite differences/high precision, verify conservation/positive dissipation/reciprocity, preserve failures and audit.
Not an AlN calculation or new physics claim. No arbitrary constant is to be presented as an absolute material scattering rate.
