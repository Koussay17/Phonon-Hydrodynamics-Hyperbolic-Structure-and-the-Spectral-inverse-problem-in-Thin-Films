# Preserved failures
1. Ordered floating-point subtraction hides +/-1 thermal detuning at huge
   energies and changes admission on daughter permutation. Frozen artifacts
   preserve it; compensated summation corrects the reproduced cases.
2. Relative resonance alone does not bound thermal detailed-balance mismatch.
3. Naive equilibrium residual/central derivative can both be zero while the
   Jacobian is wrong at very large occupations.
4. Odd-only certification misses self-reciprocal event double counting.
5. A symmetric PSD factor is not the nonlinear equilibrium Jacobian of a
   detuned reaction, nor proof of physical event support.
6. Exact zero modes and repeated daughter multiplicities are not handled by
   the prototype; pretending otherwise would erase assumptions.
7. Visual PDF inspection failed because the image tool's Windows sandbox
   initialization failed. Rendering/compilation alone is not visual review.
