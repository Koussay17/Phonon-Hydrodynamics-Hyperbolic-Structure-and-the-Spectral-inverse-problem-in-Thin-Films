# Independent hostile peer review — completed 27 September 2026

**Status: COMPLETE, bounded review of the frozen candidate.** This completes this reviewer assignment, not the material-validation programme. I read the candidate, note 22, first/second-generation derivation and verification reports, source/literature traces, and material provenance. No other red-team report was consulted; no new material calculation or full reproduction was performed. The current GitHub snapshot is not scientific acceptance.

**Verdict:** the restricted counting and moment identities withstand this review. They do not establish a physical AlN collision action, a linewidth correction, or a failure of bulk phonon kinetic theory. I would reject a standalone original-material-physics paper on this evidence: the present contribution is a conditional normalization/closure audit with one selected tensor consistency check. The note already disclaims novelty and most broader interpretations; the objections below delimit what cannot be promoted from it.

## Strongest parts and evidence classification

- **PROVED within the stipulated algebraic models:** the 6V/3V monomial factors, repeated-mode factorial factors, conditional rank-one entropy factorization, and instantaneous geometric-family tangency defect. These are finite identities, not a proof of the kinetic assumptions. Independently, the declared jump generator gives d<E[Y(Y-1)]>/dt = 2(6a+1)J at a geometric product, whereas the geometric tangent is 8aJ; their difference is indeed 2(2a+1)J.
- **NUMERICALLY DEMONSTRATED in the supplied record, not replayed here:** selected AlN permutation/conjugation residuals and the thresholded first-leg detuning statistic. No experimental support or formal verification is supplied.
- **UNSUPPORTED:** treating the factor two as a measured decay-rate change, treating the finite-channel defect as a quantitative AlN closure error, or inferring a complete material importer. The frozen candidate does not assert these stronger conclusions.

## Fatal issues, if any

No fatal counterexample found to the narrowly stated finite identities. The absence of a justified material kinetic limit is fatal to presenting them as a validated AlN dynamics result. That distinction must survive every later summary, title, and imported operator.

## Major issues

1. **The irreversible model remains an assumption.** A correctly normalized cubic Hamiltonian does not select the declared Fock Markov generator or its spectral density. A finite resonant channel supports coherent dynamics; neither its ladder factors nor detailed balance supplies the discarded time scales and coherences. The proposed tensor/Sym^2 channel operator is Hamiltonian bookkeeping. The Fock-sector alternative is exact only for its assumed stochastic model. Neither candidate direction presently resolves this physical selection problem. The primary quantum-Boltzmann master-equation literature explicitly requires a Markov approximation and an effectively continuous spectrum, then separately discards coherences ([Jaksch, Gardiner and Zoller, sections II.1.2–II.1.3](https://arxiv.org/html/quant-ph/9701008v2)).

2. **Noninvariance is not a controlled error estimate for kinetic closure.** The first-order moment defect rules out exact invariance of this finite model's product-geometric family. It does not measure the error after thermodynamic/weak-coupling scaling, many-channel averaging, or finite observation-time coarse graining. No small parameter, error norm, or limit connects the defect to AlN. Spohn's kinetic treatment explicitly distinguishes approximate local Gaussian statistics at kinetic scales from exact Gaussian preservation (section 4), and uses a closure assumption in the derivation (section 6). Thus finite noninvariance does not contradict that programme ([Spohn, inspected preprint](https://arxiv.org/html/math-ph/0505025v2)).

3. **The factor-of-two comparison is not an observable prediction.** It compares a full derivative of a conditional mean model with a source-formula equilibrium one-particle linewidth contribution. The former is not demonstrated to govern an autonomous exact response; the latter is not independently derived here as the spectral pole of this same finite repeated-mode model. Initial slopes, population-Jacobian diagonals, relaxation eigenvalues and spectral poles are distinct quantities. The optional Lindblad illustration cannot close this evidential gap. There is no demonstrated phono3py defect or physical daughter lifetime correction.

4. **The importance of identical full-mode daughters is unknown.** Equality of frequencies is insufficient. The exceptional coincident-label sector may have vanishing or non-negligible weight depending on the actual resonance-constrained collision measure and singularities. Ordinary Lebesgue measure counting before imposing the energy delta is inadequate. No material measure analysis or mesh scaling establishes its contribution. An order-one ratio for one such channel can therefore coexist with an undetectable total effect; the record does not decide.

5. **The AlN bridge is a consistency test on shared inputs.** Roundoff permutation/sewing residuals constrain implementation consistency, not force-constant accuracy, correct global event weights, a converged spectral measure, or transport. The 1472-entry statistic uses one selected triplet, one orientation and a relative threshold. Its absence of exact first-leg resonance neither excludes bulk scattering nor supplies an exactly conservative import. Global enumeration, energy integration, material convergence and a degeneracy certificate remain absent. The proposed positive matrix cannot fill these gaps by its positivity alone.

6. **Novelty and standalone significance remain unestablished.** Ordered-boson counting, factorial/Wick moments, hierarchy closure and population-versus-spectral distinctions are established structures. L/L2 correctly avoid a novelty claim, and their bounded search does not establish historical priority for the exact repeated-channel comparison. The value demonstrated is internal convention control. No new measurable material consequence is established.

## Minor issues

- The note's two-paper bibliography does not expose the closure literature used in its new statistical discussion. L2 is necessary context; its inaccessible Spohn erratum limits any use of that source's corrected detailed formulas. I did not obtain the erratum and rely only on the inspected setup/approximation statements.
- “Four independent approaches” describes different algebraic checks sharing the normalization and physical assumptions; it is not four independent physical validations.
- The provenance manifest records an absolute D-drive scratch array and SHA256. This supports local traceability, but public retrievability and a clean-environment replay were not established here. Near-machine-precision residuals are not physical uncertainty estimates.

## Decisive tests

For broader acceptance, the missing evidence is: a declared microscopic-to-kinetic limit with a quantitative closure-error test; same-model spectral and population responses rather than a diagonal/prefactor comparison; and a converged, globally enumerated AlN resonance measure quantifying the coincident-label sector. These are falsification requirements, not results of this review. A preserved-input replay would address the separate reproducibility question.

## Unresolved questions

Which AlN regime justifies the proposed Markov generator? Does the repeated-label sector carry appreciable converged collision weight? Which observable would distinguish the proposed closure from existing kinetics? Does either alternative formulation provide an error-controlled material reduction? None is answered by the present evidence.
