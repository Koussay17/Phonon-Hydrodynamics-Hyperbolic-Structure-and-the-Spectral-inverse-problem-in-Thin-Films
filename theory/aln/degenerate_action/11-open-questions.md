# Open questions
1. Map the recovered all-incoming normal-mode amplitude tensor to explicitly oriented decay/absorption channels, including conjugation and wavevector reversal.
2. Derive repeated-mode, identical-daughter and reciprocal-event counting in a declared normalization.
3. Choose and justify a kinetic approximation for the intended AlN observable. A complex amplitude alone is not an irreversible generator.
4. Test population/trace closure for that supplied material generator, or retain the required matrix or memory variables.
5. Extend amplitude validation to other triplets, Gamma/zero modes and source conventions.
6. Establish mesh/width/cutoff, temperature, isotope/defect convergence and boundary/experimental validity.
These tasks are distinct; solving phase transport for one tuple does not solve them all.
