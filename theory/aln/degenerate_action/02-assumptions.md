# Assumptions
Finite positive-frequency mode spaces and positive temperature. Exact degeneracy is a mathematical assumption in finite proofs; numerical frequency clusters are not physical degeneracy certificates.
Polarization changes stay at the same wavevector modulo the reciprocal lattice. Interblock coherences are excluded only where the declared model says so.
The scalar event model assumes exactly resonant, distinct-index reversible Bose reactions, equal mode weights and supplied counting/prefactors. W is positive diagonal Bose variance. The general closure theorem needs a finite constant L and a full-row-rank B; entropy symmetry is an additional reversible-model assumption.
Density-matrix bath examples are separately declared conditional models, not closed-crystal phonon derivations.
Material evidence uses one nonzero-frequency all-incoming triplet, shared source normalization/reciprocal tensor and pinned3x3x3 exports. No full collision, material convergence or experimental claim.
