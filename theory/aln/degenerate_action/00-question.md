Does summing P over degenerate blocks suffice for a closed block-population operator?

Homogeneous linearized kinetics, no boundary or spatial closure asserted. Positive temperature and positive nonzero phonon frequencies; exclude translational zero modes from the toy domain. Known data are the corrected fixed-grid exports, but synthetic examples are not material counterexamples.
