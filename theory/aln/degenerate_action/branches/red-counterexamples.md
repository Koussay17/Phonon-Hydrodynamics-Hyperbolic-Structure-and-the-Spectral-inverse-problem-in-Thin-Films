# Independent counterexample audit

**Completed bounded review.** Read candidate-for-review.md and first passes A–D only. No other red reports, material runs, or numerical checks were used. The examples below are hand calculations, not AlN findings.

**Verdict:** no in-scope counterexample was found to the squared-entry obstruction, finite intertwining criterion, or exact elimination identity. The attacks below defeat stronger physical inferences; they do not contradict the candidate's explicit disclaimers.

**1. Passive covariance is not a scalar closure certificate.** Transforming amplitudes and variables together can preserve all covariance checks while a fixed interaction distinguishes members of a degenerate block. A parent doublet with only its first member coupled to daughters has a block-total derivative depending on the unresolved parent imbalance. The total is basis invariant; its dynamics is not autonomous. Thus the quoted transport residuals cannot replace BL(I-HB)=0 for a specified generator. The invariant algebra center identifies observables, not a sufficient dynamical state. A group average of an interaction need not describe that interaction.

**2. Approximate degeneracy is a singular physical assumption.** Let the harmonic doublet be omega0 I+(delta/2)sigma_z. For a positive initial occupation nI+eta sigma_x, 0<eta<n, the sigma_x signal is 2eta cos(delta t); an exactly degenerate model predicts 2eta. An arbitrarily small delta/omega0 permits an order-one discrepancy at t=pi/delta. If delta exceeds collision damping, it matters before relaxation. Consequently small frequency differences or tensor covariance residuals do not establish physical gauge freedom or justify discarding the splitting. The material splitting-versus-collision comparison remains unestablished.

**3. Phase loss does not invalidate every recorded-basis population model.** Given event incidence, counts and prefactors, P=|T|^2 specifies the stipulated incoherent population generator in that basis. The phase counterexamples cannot establish its failure without testing that population approximation. Conversely, upgrading P to ordinary Gram matrices still does not specify general matrix kinetics: take T1=H and T2=H diag(1,i), with H the normalized Hadamard matrix. Both have identical P and identical one-leg Grams. For a fixed daughter projector Y=diag(1,0) and fixed parent state u=(1,1)/sqrt(2), u†T†YTu equals 1 and 1/2, respectively. This tests contraction-map sufficiency, not inequivalence under a passive change that also rotates preparations.

**4. Exact memory does not warrant a short-memory model.** For C=epsilon[[2,1],[1,1]], retaining the first coordinate gives S(z)=z+2epsilon-epsilon^2/(z+epsilon). At z=epsilon it is 2.5epsilon, while static elimination gives 2epsilon: a persistent 20% difference in the exact/projected response ratio as epsilon tends to zero. Append an independent zero energy coordinate if required. Small entries do not make the eliminated coordinate fast. Exact elimination also retains hidden-initial-state forcing; block totals alone do not specify it.

**5. Hamiltonian/Gram routes do not supply irreversibility.** The resonant cubic Hamiltonian restricted to {|1,0,0>,|0,1,1>} is a two-state exchange. Parent population oscillates as cos²(gt/hbar), with revivals rather than exponential relaxation. A bath/continuum limit and kinetic assumptions remain indispensable; finite amplitudes alone do not select them.

**Stop criteria:** no material scalar closure without its generator-specific test; no exact-degeneracy interpretation from relative frequency tolerance alone; no density-matrix law from norms/Grams alone; no finite-memory or Hamiltonian-derived collision model without a stated approximation regime. These are unresolved prerequisites, not observed failures of an AlN calculation. No novelty or proof-by-failed-counterexample claim is made.
