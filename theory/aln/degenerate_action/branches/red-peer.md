# Independent hostile peer review

Frozen candidate: 25 September 2026. Read candidate, A–D/L, PI scope/JSON/provenance and the contraction script. No other red reports, cross-agent discussion, material runs or repository edits. Two cited primary passages were checked directly.

## Verdict and major objections

**No counterexample to the four narrowly stated candidate claims was found. Reject their promotion to a new material theory, an AlN ambiguity theorem, or a validated degenerate collision importer.** The principal weakness is the uncompleted bridge between generic algebra and actual crystal kinetics.

1. **Established mathematics is not a novel closure theory.** The constant-generator condition BL=GB is the elementary linear specialization of exact lumping. The inspected [CLUE paper, Section 2 and Supplementary Proposition II.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2004.11961v2) explicitly gives the invariant-Jacobian-subspace criterion and credits earlier work. Squaring loses interference, unitary tensor norms are invariant, and Schur elimination produces memory. The campaign correctly disclaims novelty; no top-journal theoretical advance is established by repackaging these facts. The specific finite examples demonstrate the missing implications, not a new general principle.

2. **No physical AlN generator has been specified.** C's positive resonant scalar-event network and B/D's thermal covariance model have different physical assumptions. The latter requires a reservoir/Markov model; it is not obtained merely by writing a finite cubic Hamiltonian. [Trushechkin, Sections II and III.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2103.12042v3), explicitly starts with a system–bath Hamiltonian and retains population–coherence transfer at zero Bohr frequency. This supports the warning about degeneracy; it does not derive the needed phonon collision superoperator. Applying a scalar lumpability defect to claim failure of a correct density-matrix theory would be invalid. The frozen text avoids that inference and must continue to do so.

3. **The material amplitude experiment verifies a contraction, not kinetics.** It shares the reciprocal force tensor, model, normalization and saved eigenvectors. Full-basis transport follows from completeness of the eigenbases; the script also applies the destination frequency normalization. Its 2.15e-15 residual cannot independently validate scattering physics or absolute units. The 2.46e-14 block-only residual establishes consistency with the chosen numerical block assignment for one selected triplet. It does not establish physical exact degeneracy, common physical channel prefactors, or closure of a collision action. Representativeness across triplets is untested. Globally scaled/Frobenius errors do not certify the relative accuracy of weak or cancelling components.

4. **Exact-degeneracy assumptions have no material certificate here.** Grouping frequencies within 1e-10 THz is a numerical rule, not a crystal-symmetry proof or a secular-timescale argument. Mixing equal-frequency states at different momenta is not the stated polarization freedom. Near-degenerate splittings, linewidths, dephasing and the observable/source determine whether retained coherences matter; none is evaluated for an AlN response. Dark directions in synthetic tensors therefore establish no actual long-lived material mode.

5. **The importer remains incomplete despite phase recovery.** One all-incoming tensor has no validated canonical decay/absorption orientation, repeated-leg rule, full orbit expansion, complete channel set, or conserving energy integration. Amplitudes alone do not supply the missing kinetic approximation. Conversely, the generic phase-loss examples do not show that pp is insufficient for every purpose: a stipulated fixed-basis incoherent population model can use pp with validated counting. The practical phase-retention conclusion is justified only for operations requiring the missing interference information, as the frozen candidate specifies.
