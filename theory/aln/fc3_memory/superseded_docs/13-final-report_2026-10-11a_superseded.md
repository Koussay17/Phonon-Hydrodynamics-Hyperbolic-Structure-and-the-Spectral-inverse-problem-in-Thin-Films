## 0. Declared class (every statement below is about this class)
* **Rates generated by force constants:** g_alpha = W_alpha |Phi_alpha(fc3)|^2 on fixed AlN three-phonon event sets
  (Gaussian sigma = 0.1 THz, 4 sigma, 300 K) on the 5x5x3, 7x7x5 and 9x9x6 meshes, with fc2, phonons, Bose factors
  and smearing fixed. The unknown is fc3 in the 7,752-dimensional space of P6_3mc-symmetric, permutation-symmetric,
  sum-rule-satisfying third-order force constants of the 3x3x2 supercell (456 symmetry-distinct cluster orbits).
  One fc3 tensor defines the rates on every mesh.
* **Data:** all mode-orbit lifetimes (r = diag C) within +-10 %, kappa_xx and kappa_zz within +-1 %, at 300 K, on the
  mesh(es) stated.
* **Plausibility of fc3 changes:** Frobenius bound; uniform per-cluster bound eps; calibrated per-cluster bound
  eps_o = max(k delta_o, 0.5 %), delta_o the observed difference between two first-principles workflows (E13),
  optionally with the 266 orbits on which the second workflow has no constants (cutoff) held at the floor ("cal1nz").
* All AlN numbers are numbers of these discretised operators on coarse meshes, not converged AlN values.

## 1. Answer to the decision question of paper 1
**No fc3 change within first-principles precision has been found that moves tau_mem by more than about 20 % in a
way that survives a change of Brillouin-zone sampling while keeping the static data. All large changes found
(factors 1.3–6) are artefacts of the sampling on which they were optimised. Whether the data pin tau_mem is not
established — every bound is an inner bound — and on these meshes tau_mem itself is not converged: its
discretisation error (+79 % / +116 % from 5x5x3 to 9x9x6) exceeds every robust fc3 effect found.**

* **Free rates** (memory-gap campaign): witnesses 10^2–10^5 x the reference.
* **Single mesh (5x5x3), fc3 classes** (E10–E18, E22): Frobenius ~10 %: 14 x (x), 624 x (z), by rewriting single
  long-range orbits by factors up to 43. Per-cluster eps = 0.1: at least [0.83, 1.40] (x), [0.80, 1.44] (z).
  Calibrated k = 1: [0.70, 2.35], [0.68, 2.15]; k = 2: [0.64, 3.72], [0.61, 5.96]; cal1nz: [0.74, 1.86], [0.73, 1.82].
  These are properties of the 5x5x3 event operator.
* **Mesh transfer** (E19; review 4.2): the same tensors on 7x7x5 give tau ratios 0.83 / 1.08 (k = 1 maxima),
  0.71 / 0.53 (k = 2 maxima) and violate kappa by up to 30 % and single lifetimes by up to +338 %.
* **Data on two meshes** (E20): with the data imposed on 5x5x3 and 7x7x5 simultaneously, tau on 7x7x5 can still be
  raised to 1.80 (x) and 3.05 (z) in the cal1nz class, and to 1.33 / 1.69 in the per-cluster class, while 5x5x3 tau
  moves by <= 18 %.
* **Validation on an unused mesh** (E21, 9x9x6): those witnesses give 1.18, 0.97, 1.18, 1.14 and violate the data
  again (kappa by up to 8 %, lifetimes by up to +56 %). The second workflow (ALAMODE fc3) gives 0.98 / 0.93 with
  kappa within 0.6 %.
* **Second workflow at three meshes:** tau ratio -0.8 % / +1.7 % (5x5x3), -2.3 % / +0.6 % (7x7x5), -2.2 % / -6.8 %
  (9x9x6).
* **Lower end:** rigorous floor tau >= (1 - dK) tau_CS (S3a) on every mesh; 5x5x3 minima within 4–55 % of it.

## 2. Statements and status
**S1 (fc3 route reproduces the reference) — NUMERICALLY DEMONSTRATED.** W pp reproduces the memory-gap rates to
1.3e-15 (5x5x3) and 1.4e-15 (7x7x5); the amplitude engine agrees with phono3py's Rust kernels to 6.5e-12 (E1–E2).
Exact linearisations: 5x5x3 (2,712 rows) and 7x7x5 (23,313 rows, Phi0 = G c0 to 1.6e-10); the 7x7x5 linear model
reproduces phono3py-route tau and kappa ratios at independent tensors to <= 5e-5 (E19).

**S2 (local controllability, 5x5x3) — NUMERICALLY DEMONSTRATED.** Rate Jacobian rank 2,113 = number of independent
block rates (E6); the 335 selection-rule orbits stay zero for every symmetric fc3.

**S3 (rigorous statements).** (a) tau >= K/|b|^2 >= (1 - dK) tau_CS for any admissible rates — PROVED (memory-gap
P4(a), audited there). (b) Frobenius-ball ceiling — PROVED (memory-gap P7); vacuous from rho = 0.5 % on (E7). No
rigorous upper bound exists for the per-cluster or calibrated classes; all maxima are inner bounds.

**S4 (single-mesh fc3 classes) — NUMERICALLY DEMONSTRATED as properties of the 5x5x3 event operator; not
properties of AlN.** Witnesses E10, E15–E18, E22, each verified with phono3py amplitudes. Every inner solve was
budget-limited (09 item 16), so the ranges are inner bounds.

**S5 (sampling artefact) — NUMERICALLY DEMONSTRATED.** Every single-mesh witness tested on a finer mesh loses or
reverses its memory change and violates the data there (E19; independently, review 4.2 with phono3py's own LBTE).
Two-mesh witnesses lose most of their change on a third mesh and violate the data there (E20–E21).

**S6 (no robust large change found) — NUMERICAL OBSERVATION.** On a mesh unused in the optimisation, the largest
tau change of any witness is 1.18 x, and every such witness violates the data on that mesh. This is absence of a
counterexample within the searches made, not a bound.

**S7 (identifiability of tau_mem from fc3-level data) — UNRESOLVED.** Neither "the data pin tau_mem within ~20 %"
nor its negation is established. A decision requires constraints imposed on a converged (or sampling-independent)
operator, which these meshes do not provide (S8).

**S8 (discretisation dominates) — NUMERICALLY DEMONSTRATED for these meshes.** Reference tau_x 34.25 / 52.61 / 61.40
ps and tau_z 27.45 / 50.62 / 59.15 ps on 5x5x3 / 7x7x5 / 9x9x6 at sigma = 0.1 THz (kappa_zz 202.0 / 272.3 / 256.9,
non-monotone). tau_mem is not mesh-converged.

**S9 (calibration) — mapping NUMERICALLY DEMONSTRATED; delta_o partly an artefact.** The ALAMODE tensor maps into the
symmetric space to 5e-14; two maps differing by a crystal symmetry agree to 1.2e-16 (E13); an independent
re-derivation agrees to 2.1e-16 and the same conventions map ALAMODE's fc2 onto phono3py's to 0.72 % (review 1.1).
On 266 of 456 orbits delta_o = 1 is ALAMODE's cutoff, not a disagreement (09 item 17). One workflow pair only.

**S10 (local identifiability of short-range fc3 at 7x7x5) — NUMERICALLY DEMONSTRATED at first order** (rank 218/218
in the 3.5 A subspace; E14).

**S11 (sensitivity, task 2) — NUMERICAL OBSERVATIONS** (E3–E5), unchanged.

**S12 (convention dependence) — NUMERICALLY DEMONSTRATED (review 2.2).** The 5x5x3 witnesses are data-consistent in
the campaign's conserving finite-sigma discretisation and violate kappa by 0.2–3.2 points in phono3py's own LBTE;
the difference is the weighting of detuned events. Reference values agree between the two to 1e-4 / 1.1e-3.

## 3. Fragile points and scope
1. All maxima and minima are local and budget-limited; S6 is a statement about the searches made.
2. Three meshes, one smearing (sigma = 0.1 THz). The memory time is far from mesh convergence (S8); the
   identifiability question is posed about operators that change by more than the effect under study.
3. One pair of workflows for the calibration; the data tolerance (+-10 % lifetimes) is tighter than the
   inter-workflow spread (18–28 %), so the declared data class is stricter than first-principles precision.
4. fc2 fixed; harmonic uncertainty not propagated.
5. Data at 300 K only; the 5x5x3 witnesses are also excluded by kappa(T) (review 4.3: kappa at 100 K off by +23 % to
   +217 %).

## 4. Conclusion for paper 1
The free-rate memory gap is an artefact of ignoring the amplitude structure, and the large fc3-level ambiguities
that a single-mesh analysis seems to find are artefacts of the Brillouin-zone sampling: they disappear on a mesh not
used to construct them, where they also break the data. What survives is (i) no demonstrated fc3-driven ambiguity of
tau_mem beyond about 20 %, with the identification question itself unresolved, and (ii) a discretisation dependence
of tau_mem (+79 % / +116 % between 5x5x3 and 9x9x6 at sigma = 0.1 THz) larger than any fc3 effect. For a conserving
closure of AlN this means: tau_mem from first principles is plausible but must be quoted with a convergence study in
mesh and smearing; quoting it from one coarse mesh is not justified. The natural next steps are the convergence
study of tau_mem itself, and only then the identifiability question on the converged operator (constraints imposed
in a sampling-independent form, a third workflow, kappa(T) as data).
