## 0. Declared class (every statement below is about this class)
* **Rates generated by force constants:** g_alpha = W_alpha |Phi_alpha(fc3)|^2 on fixed AlN three-phonon event sets
  (Gaussian sigma = 0.1 THz, 4 sigma, 300 K) on Gamma-centred meshes 5x5x3 to 12x12x8, fc2, phonons, Bose factors and
  smearing fixed. The unknown is fc3 in the 7,752-dimensional space of P6_3mc-symmetric, permutation-symmetric,
  sum-rule-satisfying third-order force constants of the 3x3x2 supercell (456 symmetry-distinct cluster orbits). One
  fc3 tensor defines the rates on every mesh.
* **Data (synthetic):** the reference tensor's own predictions on the same mesh — all mode-orbit lifetimes
  (r = diag C, entropy coordinates) within +-10 % and kappa_xx, kappa_zz within +-1 %, at 300 K. This is richer than
  any measurement (it contains the long-wavelength acoustic lifetimes) and tighter than the spread between two
  first-principles workflows (ALAMODE vs phono3py: lifetimes up to 18–28 %, kappa_zz up to 3.3 %).
* **Plausibility of fc3 changes:** Frobenius bound; uniform per-cluster bound eps; calibrated per-cluster bound
  eps_o = max(k delta_o, 0.5 %), delta_o the difference between the phono3py and ALAMODE tensors of the Phonon
  Olympics benchmark (one workflow pair from one benchmark protocol; not a first-principles uncertainty model),
  optionally with the 266 orbits beyond ALAMODE's cutoff held at the floor ("cal1nz").
* All AlN numbers are numbers of these discretised operators, not converged AlN values (section 1, item 1).

## 1. Answer to the decision question of paper 1
**The question cannot be answered as posed, because the posed quantity is not under control: the bulk memory time
tau_mem = N/K is carried by the long-wavelength acoustic phonons, does not converge with Brillouin-zone refinement on
any mesh computed, and may be infrared-divergent. On every discretised operator the static data leave tau_mem
undetermined by large factors; one mechanism of this freedom — slowing the long-wavelength c-axis transverse acoustic
modes — persists across three meshes, but every tensor found violates the data on a mesh not used in its
construction. Identifiability of tau_mem from static data is UNRESOLVED.**

1. **tau_mem is an infrared quantity (E23, E24).**
   * Event operator, sigma = 0.1 THz: tau_x 34.3 / 45.1 / 52.6 / 61.4 / 73.6 ps on 5x5x3 / 6x6x4 / 7x7x5 / 9x9x6 /
     12x12x8 (tau_z 27.4 ... 70.1 ps); no convergent regime (observed order -0.07 ... -0.17). The linear algebra is
     converged (cond <= 351, regulariser-independent to 1e-13).
   * RTA, phono3py tetrahedron: tau_mem,x 60.2 / 69.7 / 135.4 ps on 9x9x5 / 15x15x9 / 31x31x17 (the benchmark grid),
     while kappa changes by 11 %.
   * The part of the memory moment from modes above 3 THz is mesh-converged (x 31.1 / 31.6 / 31.7 ps, z 27.0 / 26.9 /
     27.8 ps on 15x15x9 / 19x19x11 / 31x31x17); all mesh dependence lies below 3 THz. On 31x31x17 one star of
     longitudinal acoustic modes (f = 1.29 THz, tau = 1.06 ns) supplies 21 % of tau_mem,x.
   * A long-wavelength law gamma ~ f^n makes N diverge as f_min -> 0 iff n >= 3/2 (K converges for n < 3). Measured
     effective exponents straddle 3/2 (TA 1.1–1.4 below 2 THz; LA 2.3–2.9 over 1.5–4 THz; diag C 2.0 -> 1.6 from
     7x7x5 to 12x12x8) in pre-asymptotic windows. Existence of the bulk limit: UNRESOLVED; divergence: PLAUSIBLE.
2. **Single-mesh freedom (E10–E18, E22).** On 5x5x3: Frobenius ~10 %: 14 x / 624 x; per-cluster eps = 0.1: at least
   [0.83, 1.40] (x), [0.80, 1.44] (z); calibrated k = 1: [0.70, 2.35], [0.68, 2.15]; k = 2: [0.64, 3.72],
   [0.61, 5.96]; cal1nz: [0.74, 1.86], [0.73, 1.82]. With the data imposed on 5x5x3 and 7x7x5 together (E20), tau on
   7x7x5 still reaches 1.80 (x) and 3.05 (z) (cal1nz) and 1.33 / 1.69 (per-cluster). Budget-limited inner bounds (near
   first-order KKT points for E20). These are properties of the discretised operators.
3. **Transfer to meshes not used in construction (E19, E21, E24).**
   * Calibrated-class and two-mesh witnesses lose or reverse their effect: k = 1 maxima 2.35 / 2.15 -> 0.83 / 1.08
     (7x7x5); k = 2 maxima -> 0.71 / 0.53; E20 witnesses 1.80 / 3.05 / 1.33 / 1.69 -> 1.18 / 0.97 / 1.18 / 1.14
     (9x9x6). Mechanism: one engineered slow eigenpair among the objective mesh's Gamma-adjacent modes (58–88 % of N).
   * Per-cluster c-axis witnesses keep and increase their effect: 1.37 -> 1.35 -> 1.52 and 1.44 -> 1.32 -> 1.60
     (5x5x3 / 7x7x5 / 9x9x6). Mechanism: lower rates of the long-wavelength c-axis TA modes (a diagonal lever).
   * **Every witness violates the declared data on a mesh not used in its construction** (kappa by 1.8–30 %, lifetimes
     by up to +338 %; the persistent c-axis witnesses: kappa_zz +5–6 %, lifetimes to -37 % on 9x9x6). So does the
     second-workflow tensor (lifetimes up to 28 %, kappa_zz +3.3 % on 5x5x3), whose tau stays within 7 % of the
     reference on three meshes (-0.8 / +1.7 %, -2.3 / +0.6 %, -2.2 / -6.8 %).
4. **Lower end:** tau >= (1 - dK) tau_CS on every mesh (proved); this floor allows ratios down to 0.31–0.40 on 7x7x5
   and 9x9x6; no robust minimisation was run.

## 2. Statements and status
**S1 (fc3 route) — NUMERICALLY DEMONSTRATED.** W pp reproduces the event rates to 1.3e-15; independent amplitude
engine vs phono3py Rust kernels 6.5e-12 (E1–E2). Exact linearisations at 5x5x3 and 7x7x5 (Phi0 = G c0 to 1.6e-10
row-wise, 1e-13 relative to max |Phi0|); linear model vs phono3py route: amplitudes 1.5e-11, tau ratios <= 6e-5 (E19)
and <= 1.8e-4 (E20), the difference being the block- vs orbit-average convention (E24).

**S2 (local controllability, 5x5x3) — NUMERICALLY DEMONSTRATED** (E6, first order).

**S3 (rigorous statements).** (a) tau >= K/|b|^2 >= (1 - dK) tau_CS — PROVED (memory-gap P4(a), audited there).
(b) Frobenius-ball ceiling — PROVED (memory-gap P7), vacuous from rho = 0.5 % (E7). No rigorous upper bound for the
per-cluster or calibrated classes.

**S4 (single-mesh and two-mesh ranges) — NUMERICALLY DEMONSTRATED as budget-limited inner bounds on the stated
discretised operators** (section 1, item 2).

**S5 (transfer) — NUMERICALLY DEMONSTRATED for the listed tensors.** Calibrated-class and two-mesh witnesses are
mesh-specific; the per-cluster c-axis effect persists through 9x9x6; every witness violates the data on an unused mesh
(section 1, item 3). Whether a tensor exists that keeps the data on several meshes and changes tau robustly was not
decided: no search targeted it, and the transfer test has no measured power (review M2).

**S6 (infrared control) — NUMERICALLY DEMONSTRATED (event operator to 12x12x8; RTA to 31x31x17).** tau_mem is
dominated by Gamma-adjacent acoustic modes (< 0.5 % of the modes carry 25–65 % of N) and does not converge with the
mesh; the part above 3 THz converges (section 1, item 1).

**S7 (existence of a bulk limit) — UNRESOLVED; infrared divergence PLAUSIBLE.**

**S8 (identifiability of bulk tau_mem from static data) — UNRESOLVED,** and possibly ill-posed (S7).

**S9 (calibration) — mapping NUMERICALLY DEMONSTRATED** (two symmetry-related maps agree to 1.2e-16; independent
re-derivation 2.1e-16; fc2 mapped with the same conventions agrees to 0.72 %). delta_o = 1 on 266 cutoff orbits is not
a measured difference. One workflow pair, one benchmark protocol (the review reports a common functional for all
teams; not checked here), fc2 fixed.

**S10 (local identifiability of short-range fc3, 7x7x5, 3.5 A subspace) — NUMERICALLY DEMONSTRATED at first order,
one mesh:** rank 218/218, sigma_min 0.118 (condition 1.9e3): a 1 % data change allows up to about 8.5 % change of the
short-range constants (weak practical identifiability).

**S11 (sensitivity, E3–E5) — NUMERICAL OBSERVATIONS,** unchanged.

**S12 (convention dependence) — NUMERICALLY DEMONSTRATED.** Detuned-event weighting: the 5x5x3 witnesses violate
kappa by 0.2–3.2 points in phono3py's own LBTE (review 2.2); not tested for E20. Orbit vs block averaging: 1–5 % for
z quantities on even c-meshes (E24). Smearing at fixed mesh: tau_x changes by 38 % on 7x7x5 between sigma = 0.05 and
0.1 THz, 1.6 % on 12x12x8 (E24).

## 3. Fragile points and scope
1. All extrema are local, single-start (E20) and budget-limited; S5 concerns the tensors found.
2. The meshes computed are far from resolving the long-wavelength acoustic modes that carry tau_mem (S6).
3. One calibration pair; data class synthetic and stricter than first-principles precision; fc2 fixed.
4. Data at 300 K only; the 5x5x3 witnesses are excluded by kappa(T) (review 4.3); E20 and the persistent c-axis
   witnesses were not tested against kappa(T).
5. Gaussian smearing only for the event operator; tetrahedron only at RTA level (E23).

## 4. Conclusion for paper 1
1. The memory time of the bulk conserving closure is controlled by long-wavelength acoustic phonons and is not
   converged on any Brillouin-zone mesh used, including the benchmark grid 31x31x17 at RTA level; it may not exist as
   a bulk quantity. Paper 1 should therefore not quote a bulk first-principles tau_mem for AlN.
2. The well-posed objects are a regularised memory time: (i) the memory moment above a frequency cutoff, which is
   mesh-converged (31.7 / 27.8 ps above 3 THz, RTA); (ii) tau_mem with boundary scattering at the film thickness of the
   thesis setting, the physical regulator of the FDTR problem; (iii) the low-frequency asymptotic coefficient of the
   acoustic linewidths, if a power law is established.
3. On each discretised operator the static data leave tau_mem undetermined by large factors (S4). The one freedom
   that persists across meshes acts on the long-wavelength acoustic linewidths, the same modes that control tau_mem,
   and it is excluded on finer meshes by the data only because the synthetic data class contains those modes'
   lifetimes. With realistic data (kappa(T), a few measured linewidths) these modes are not constrained directly.
   The free-rate gap of the memory-gap campaign is not closed by the amplitude structure alone (E10: 14 x / 624 x);
   only a per-cluster prior narrows it, on a given mesh.
4. Next steps, in order: the infrared law of the acoustic linewidths at 300 K (fine q-lines Gamma–A, Gamma–M,
   tetrahedron and adaptive smearing); the film-regularised tau_mem(L); the identifiability question for that quantity,
   with constraints imposed in a sampling-independent form, at least three workflows and two functionals as the
   precision model, kappa(T) as data, and a probabilistic propagation alongside the worst-case inner bounds.
