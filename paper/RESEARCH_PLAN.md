# Manuscript scope and completion criteria

Status: working plan, not a completed manuscript or novelty claim.
Updated: 18 September 2026.

## Candidate central question
What do static thermal transport data determine about a conserving dynamic
closure, and which additional operator and measurement information is needed?

## Evidence already available
- Grey conserving derivation: note 14, within its stated asymptotic assumptions.
- Conditional identifiability and prior-work comparison: notes 7 and 15.
- Synthetic Fourier FDTR design: note 16; not laboratory validation.
- Published discrete AlN RTA illustration: note 17; one temperature/mesh.
- Audited operator-class distinction and limitations: note 18 and
  theory/aln/closure/13-final-report.md.

## Provisional paper structure
1. Question, experimental motivation, and precise relation to prior art.
2. Entropy coordinates, operator classes, observables and assumptions.
3. What diagonal rates and static response do and do not identify.
4. Fixed finite event geometry: scope and limits of the audited bound.
5. Reproducible finite examples and independent numerical checks.
6. AlN illustration: available data and explicitly missing physical inputs.
7. Consequences for inverse inference, limitations and next measurements.
8. Conclusions restricted to the evidence.

Supplement: detailed proofs, event conventions, numerical conditioning,
reproducibility environment and failed approaches that affect interpretation.

## Required before a theory/methods submission
- Targeted prior-art assessment of the actual proposed contribution.
- A clear useful contribution beyond established projection/memory theory.
- Consistent notation and explicit separation of abstract matrices from
  microscopic event generators and from material predictions.
- Full claim-to-proof/source/experiment mapping.
- Independent final review, final figures and visual PDF inspection.
- Agreement on authorship, target journal and data/code release.

## Additional requirements for a quantitative AlN/FDTR submission
- Verified material collision action, including the physical even sector.
- Consistent N/U/isotope/defect inputs and temperature/mesh convergence.
- Validated film/interface/streaming response for the actual geometry.
- Sample, instrument, calibration and laboratory measurements.
- Model comparison and uncertainty including nuisance parameters.
