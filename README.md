# ❖ I · ARGVMENTVM

### The method, and why it is not the usual one

There is a kind of computational work that proves nothing. You take a well-known
instability, you integrate it, the pictures look like the textbook, and you write
*"the simulation successfully reproduces the expected behaviour."*

It reproduces nothing. **The instability was never in doubt.** A two-stream
plasma goes unstable; a shear layer rolls up; a shock forms. Reproducing the
qualitative outcome of a phenomenon that has been understood since 1880 is not
evidence of anything.

What has evidentiary value is narrower and much harder:

<summary><b>&nbsp;I &nbsp;·&nbsp; Rebond cosmologique non singulier en gravité des cordes</b></summary>

<br>

**Question.** Can the Big Bang singularity be replaced by a bounce, using the
`O(d,d)`-invariant α′ corrections of string theory?

**Proved.** In the Hohm–Zwiebach framework, where every α′ correction collapses
into a single even function `F(H)`:

- **Consistency** — `ρ̄′ + dH·p̄ = 0` holds for *any* resummation `F`
- **No string-frame vacuum bounce** — if `H(t₀) = 0` in vacuum then `H ≡ 0`
- **Threshold theorem** — bounce iff `Φ̄′ = −H`, forcing `W(H) ≡ HF′ − F ≤ H²`,
  identically violated at leading order where `W = dH²`
- **Graceful-exit obstruction** — while `F ≥ 0`, `Φ̄′` is monotone, so the exit
  needs either `W < 0` or negative potential energy
- **Branch singularity** — blow-up at any simple zero of `F″`

```
threshold          H_thr  = 2.389862 H*      root of 3 ln(1+x) = x
bounce             |H_b|  = 1.0134 × H_thr
constraint         |C|max = 3.9 × 10⁻¹²      never imposed, diagnostic only
coupling           g_s    ≤ 8.4 × 10⁻³       perturbative throughout
past               a_E ∝ |τ|^0.4990          radiation-dominated contraction
future             a_E ∝  τ ^0.4997          radiation-dominated expansion
α′ share of the NEC-violation budget         99.83 %
```

The tree-level exotic energy requirement (`V = 11.84`) drops to `0.0203`. The
bounce is a **string effect**, quantifiably.

**Not done.** `F` is postulated, not derived from amplitudes. Perturbations are
not computed. A radiation-dominated contraction is unstable to anisotropy — the
companion notebook quantifies it: initial shear must satisfy
`(σ²/κ²ρ)_init ≲ 10⁻⁹`. That is fine-tuning, and I say so.

<summary><b>&nbsp;III &nbsp;·&nbsp; Mécanique non holonome du roulement</b></summary>

<br>

**Proved.** `[X₁,X₂] = a⁻²·E_z ∉ D`, so by Frobenius the rolling constraint is
non-integrable. Then `[X₁,E_z]` and `[X₂,E_z]` generate the rest —
bracket-generating, hence completely controllable by Chow–Rashevskii.
Lagrange–d'Alembert equations are **not variational**: the vakonomic equations
differ, and coincide only when the constraint is integrable.

The rolling cone with fixed apex is **holonomic** — its constraint integrates to
`ψ·sin α + φ = const`. *Rolling does not imply non-holonomic.*

```
𝒜/a² = 0.0025   →   Θ/𝒜 = 0.9997918          the area law
second order:  Θ = 𝒜(1 − 𝒜/12 + …)   for a square
shape dependence at equal area 0.36:
    square 0.6 × 0.6       Θ = 0.349774
    rectangle 2.4 × 0.15   Θ = 0.279577       ← non-abelian correction
reversal check   ‖R_direct · R_inverse − I‖ = 7 × 10⁻¹³
```

First order is universal — the curvature. Second order depends on loop shape:
the signature of a **non-abelian** structure group. For an abelian connection
(Aharonov–Bohm) `Θ` would be exactly proportional to area.

**The Chaplygin sleigh** is the gem: `v′ = a·ω²`, `ω′ = −(ma/J)·v·ω`, integrated
in closed form via `φ′ = −λ·sin φ`. It has an **attractor while conserving energy
to `1.8 × 10⁻¹²`**. The divergence of the vector field is `−(ma/J)·v ≠ 0`:
non-holonomic systems are neither Hamiltonian nor dissipative. A third class.

<summary><b>&nbsp;V &nbsp;·&nbsp; Le piège de Fourier des réseaux de diffraction</b></summary>

<br>

**Question.** Why did the Fourier modal method converge beautifully in TE and
catastrophically in TM for twenty years?

**Not physics, not numerics — Fourier analysis.** The Fourier series of a
*product* is not always the truncated convolution.

Li's three rules, and the diagnosis that follows in three lines from the
interface conditions (`E_y, E_z` continuous; `D_x = εE_x` continuous, hence
`E_x` **discontinuous**):

- `D_y = εE_y` — no concurrent discontinuity → **Laurent**. *This is why TE never
  had a problem.*
- `D_x = εE_x` — concurrent, complementary → **inverse rule** `[[1/ε]]⁻¹`

The subtlety that makes the error so easy: in the TM equation both products
involve the **same** factor `1/ε` and require **different** rules — because
`∂ₓH_y ∝ εE_z` is discontinuous while `∂_zH_y ∝ D_x` is continuous.

```
validation, lossless metal → energy exactly conserved
    R + T = 1.000000000000       in TM and TE, at every truncation

convergence of DE₀ (TM, metallic lamellar grating)
    N = 32   naive 1.33e-3   Li 1.58e-4   gain  8.4
    N = 48   naive 1.77e-2   Li 3.11e-4   gain 56.9  ← naive error GREW ×13
    N = 64   naive 8.20e-3   Li 1.28e-4   gain 64.2

fitted order   naive N^(+0.07)    Li N^(−1.15)    TE N^(−2.86)
same limit?    0.301692 ± 2.9e-5  vs  0.301969 ± 4.9e-4     ✓
```

**Honest ceiling.** Even corrected, convergence caps at `N^(−1.15)`, not
`N^(−3)` — that is the **Meixner edge singularity** at the metal corners, which
bounds Fourier coefficients regardless of factorization. The diagnosis names the
right remedy: not a better convolution, but adaptive spatial resolution.

</details>

**Taylor–Culick, and the mistake everyone makes.** `V = √(2σ/ρh)` is a
**momentum** balance. The energy balance gives `√(4σ/ρh)` — wrong by exactly
`√2`, measured ratio `1.414214`. Half the surface energy is dissipated at the
film/rim junction **even in a perfect fluid**: it is a perfectly inelastic
collision, and viscosity sets the thickness of the joint, not the amount
dissipated.

**The exact singularity.** In uniaxial extension Oldroyd-B has
`A_xx = 1/(1−2Wi)` and `η_E = 3η_s + 3η_p/[(1−2Wi)(1+Wi)]`, which **diverges at
`Wi = ½`**. Beyond it, exponential growth at rate exactly `(2Wi−1)/λ`.

```
steady state      A_xx at Wi = 0.45     10.000000  vs  10.000000 exact
extensional visc. η_E at Wi = 0.48      45.908103  vs  45.908108 exact
blow-up rate      Wi = 1.0               1.00000000 vs  1.00000000 exact
FENE-P saturation A_xx → L²(1−1/2Wi)     499.833   vs  500 predicted
```

**The real numerical failure mode.** The conformation tensor is a covariance
matrix — it *must* stay positive definite. Explicit Euler, relaxing from
`A₀ = 50`, goes **negative in a single step** once `Δt/λ > 1`. That is the
high-Weissenberg-number problem that blocked computational rheology for twenty
years. Log-conformation fixes it *by construction* — but is **not more
accurate**, a nuance the paper states.

**The conclusion, and it is negative.** The rim strain rate is `ε̇ ~ V/h`, so the
threshold is crossed once `λ > λ_c = h/(2V) ∝ h^(3/2)`. For a 10 µm water film,
`λ_c = 1.3 µs`. Real relaxation times are **three to seven orders of magnitude
larger**. Using Oldroyd-B there is not an approximation — it is a category error.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>&nbsp;IX &nbsp;·&nbsp; Turbulence : ce qui est démontré et ce qui ne l'est pas</b></summary>

<br>

**Proved.** Buckingham's theorem by a rank argument (`dim ker D = n − r`), which
gives K41 in one line. Then the only exact non-trivial result in the field —
Kármán–Howarth–Monin → the 4/3 law → Kolmogorov's **4/5 law**,
`⟨δu_L³⟩ = −(4/5)εr`, hence `ζ₃ = 1` exactly. No similarity hypothesis. The
**negative sign** is the physical content: it encodes the direction of the
cascade.

**Assumed.** The dissipative anomaly: `ε` finite as `ν → 0`, requiring
`⟨|∇u|²⟩ → ∞` like `1/ν`. Developed turbulence is a **singular** object in the
inviscid limit.

**Wrong in detail.** K41 for `p ≠ 3`, by Landau's objection. The real exponents
are **anomalous dimensions** — not by analogy, in the exact sense of field
theory. Nobody can compute them.

```
validation first   nonlinear term conserves energy       6.3 × 10⁻¹³
                   and generalized helicity              1.8 × 10⁻¹³

diagnosed artifact GOY period-3 oscillation, 35 % modulation
                   uncorrected   ζ₃ = 0.896   10 % error on an EXACT exponent
                   corrected     ζ₃ = 0.98397  1.60 %   ← triad geometric mean

spectrum           E(k) ∝ k^(−1.702)   not −5/3, and that is CORRECT
```

The shell model is *more* intermittent than real turbulence — reducing to one
amplitude per octave removes geometry. It reproduces the **existence** of the
anomaly, not its value. Stated in the paper.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>&nbsp;X &nbsp;·&nbsp; Lois de conservation hyperboliques</b></summary>

<br>

**Classical solutions die.** `t_b = −1/min u₀′`, from the vanishing of the
characteristic Jacobian `1 + t·u₀′`. For `u₀ = −sin πx`: `t_b = 1/π`, verified to
`3 × 10⁻⁶ %`. The initial datum is analytic — the singularity is made by the
equation.

**Weak solutions are not unique** — *exhibited*, not invoked. For Burgers with
`u_L = 0`, `u_R = 1`, both the rarefaction fan and the "expansion shock" of speed
`s = ½` are weak solutions, and the second satisfies Rankine–Hugoniot exactly.

**The second law selects.** Along the Hugoniot curve,
`Δs/c_v = ln P + γ·ln[(βP+1)/(P+β)]` is strictly negative for `P < 1`.

**A derived corollary.** Expanding in `P = 1+ε`, the `ε` *and* `ε²` terms cancel
**exactly** (because `a−b = −1/γ` and `a+b = 1`), leaving
`Δs/c_v = (γ²−1)/(12γ²)·ε³`. Coefficient `0.04081633` for `γ = 1.4`; measured
ratio `0.9955` at `ε = 0.003`. **A weak shock is almost isentropic.**

```
exact Riemann solver, Sod    p* = 0.303130178051   u* = 0.927452620049
Rankine–Hugoniot in the shock frame
    mass      ρv           discrepancy  5.6 × 10⁻¹⁷
    momentum  p + ρv²      discrepancy  1.1 × 10⁻¹⁶
    enthalpy  h + v²/2     discrepancy  8.9 × 10⁻¹⁶
entropy       +0.054 at the shock,  exactly 0 across the rarefaction

nozzle        A/A*(0.999) = A/A*(1.001) = 1.00000083   → M = 1 at a minimum
shock position  p_b/p₀ = 0.5  →  A_s/A_t = 2.460494,  M₁ = 2.4256
```

**The throat is a saddle point.** Two branches pass through `M = 1`; a generic
flow does not transit, only a precise back pressure selects the separatrix.
*That* is sonic choking.

**Open problem flagged.** In several space dimensions, uniqueness of admissible
weak solutions is **not** proved — De Lellis and Székelyhidi showed persistent
non-uniqueness for Euler.

</details>
